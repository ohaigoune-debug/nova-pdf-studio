Letters of application and CVs

Objective: Write a formal letter of application and a short CV, respecting their layout, sections and polite formal language.

In two lines: A letter of application presents you and asks for a job or a place in a training programme. A CV lists your personal details, education, skills and experience in a clear, organised way.

🔵 Rules and concepts

  1. Layout: your address and the date at the top right; the recipient's address on the left, below.
  2. Salutation and ending: Dear Sir or Madam → Yours faithfully; Dear Mr Brown → Yours sincerely.
  3. Opening paragraph: say why you are writing and where you saw the advertisement: I am writing to apply for the post of…
  4. Body: describe your qualifications, experience and qualities: I have experience in…, I am fluent in…
  5. Closing paragraph: availability and polite request: I look forward to hearing from you.
  6. Formal style: no contractions (I am, not I'm), no slang, short clear paragraphs.
  7. CV: headings, short notes instead of full sentences, and the most recent information first.

🟢 Examples

  1. Informal: I want the job you put in the paper. Formal answer: I am writing to apply for the position advertised in yesterday's newspaper.
  2. Informal: Write back soon! Formal answer: I look forward to hearing from you.
  3. CV line for skills: Answer: Languages: Arabic (native), French and English (good); IT: word processing.

🟠 Common BAC mistakes

  • Ending Dear Sir or Madam with Yours sincerely.
  • Using contractions and informal phrases such as Hi or Bye.
  • Forgetting to mention the job and where it was advertised.
  • Writing the CV as a long paragraph instead of headings.

🟡 Quick review

  • Addresses + date + salutation + 3 paragraphs + ending.
  • Sir or Madam → faithfully; name → sincerely.
  • Formal language, no contractions.
  • CV = clear headings and short notes.
