The passive voice

Objective: To transform active sentences into the passive and to use the passive to describe actions related to waste and recycling when the doer is unknown or unimportant.

The idea in two lines: In the passive, the object of the active sentence becomes the subject. We form it with be + past participle, keeping the tense of the active verb in the verb be.

🔵 Rules and concepts

  1. Form: subject + be (in the tense of the active verb) + past participle (+ by agent).
  2. Present simple: am / is / are + PP: Paper is collected every week.
  3. Past simple: was / were + PP: The bins were emptied yesterday.
  4. Present perfect: has / have been + PP; future: will be + PP; modals: must / should / can be + PP.
  5. The agent (by + doer) is omitted when it is unknown, obvious or unimportant (people, someone, they).
  6. Use: the passive is common in scientific and environmental texts because the focus is on the action.

🟢 Examples

  1. Turn into the passive: "People throw tons of food away." Answer: Tons of food are thrown away. Explanation: present simple; "by people" is dropped.
  2. "The municipality built a recycling centre." Answer: A recycling centre was built by the municipality.
  3. "We must reduce waste." Answer: Waste must be reduced.

🟠 Common BAC mistakes

  • Changing the tense: the tense of be must match the active verb.
  • Forgetting be: Bottles recycled ✗ → Bottles are recycled ✓.
  • Wrong past participle of irregular verbs: throwed ✗ → thrown ✓.
  • Keeping by people / by someone: it adds nothing.

🟡 Quick review

  • Object → subject.
  • be (same tense) + past participle.
  • Modal + be + PP.
  • Agent with by, only if it matters.
  • Check agreement: is / are, was / were.
