The gerund after verbs of preference

Goal: To use the gerund (verb + -ing) correctly after verbs of preference such as like, love, enjoy, prefer, hate, can't stand and don't mind, in order to talk about your tastes, hobbies and the job you would prefer.

The idea in two lines: A gerund is the -ing form of a verb used like a noun: reading, swimming. After verbs that express what we like or dislike, the second verb takes the gerund: I enjoy reading, not I enjoy read.

🔵 Rules and concepts

  1. Gerund = verb + -ing used as a noun. It can be a subject (Swimming is good for health) or an object (I like swimming).
  2. Verbs of preference + gerund: like, love, enjoy, adore, prefer, dislike, hate, can't stand, don't mind, be fond of, be keen on, be interested in.
  3. Spelling rules: final silent -e disappears (write → writing); one vowel + one consonant → double the consonant (run → running, sit → sitting); -ie → -ying (lie → lying); -y stays (play → playing).
  4. Comparing preferences: prefer + gerund + to + gerund: I prefer walking to driving. Also I'd rather + base verb: I'd rather walk.
  5. Negative form: put not before the gerund or use the negative of the main verb: I don't like getting up early.
  6. Questions about tastes: Do you like playing chess? What do you enjoy doing at weekends?
  7. After a preposition, the verb is also a gerund: She is good at singing. He is interested in coding.

🟢 Examples

  1. Complete: Salim can't stand ........ (wait) in queues, but he doesn't mind ........ (help) his mother at home. Solution: can't stand waiting ... doesn't mind helping. Explanation: both are verbs of preference, so both take -ing.
  2. Write a sentence with prefer ... to: Meriem / read novels / watch TV. Solution: Meriem prefers reading novels to watching TV.
  3. Correct the spelling: I love swiming and danceing. Solution: I love swimming and dancing. Explanation: double the m in swim; drop the e in dance.

🟠 Common BEM mistakes

  • Base verb after the preference verb: I enjoy play football ✗ → playing ✓.
  • prefer ... than ✗ → prefer reading to watching ✓.
  • Spelling: writeing, runing, studing ✗ → writing, running, studying ✓.
  • Forgetting the -s on the main verb with he/she: She enjoy singing ✗ → She enjoys singing ✓.

🟡 Quick review

  • like / love / enjoy / hate / can't stand / don't mind + verb-ing.
  • prefer + -ing + to + -ing; would rather + base verb.
  • Drop silent -e, double the final consonant, -ie → -ying.
  • Gerund after prepositions: good at, interested in, fond of.
  • Gerund can be a subject: Reading is fun.
