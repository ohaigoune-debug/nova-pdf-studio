Prepositions of place and location

Goal: By the end of this lesson, you will be able to say where a landmark is located using prepositions of place (in, on, at, next to, between, opposite, in front of, behind) and to locate a city on a map (in the north of, on the coast).

The idea in two lines: A preposition of place links a thing to the place where it is. In is for a country, a city or an enclosed space; on for a surface, a river or a coast; at for a precise point or an address.

🔵 Rules and concepts

  1. In: countries, cities and enclosed spaces. Timgad is in Algeria. The mosaics are in the museum.
  2. On: surfaces, streets, rivers and coasts. The mosque is on Didouche Mourad Street. Cairo is on the Nile.
  3. At: a precise point or an address. Meet me at the entrance. The library is at 12 Liberty Street.
  4. Position words: next to, between … and …, opposite, in front of, behind, near, far from, above, under.
  5. On a map: in the east of a country (inside it), but to the east of another place (outside it). Constantine is in the east of Algeria. Tunisia is to the east of Algeria.
  6. Locating a landmark: be located / situated + preposition. The Great Pyramid is located near Cairo. Question: Where is it located?

🟢 Examples

  1. Complete: The Martyrs' Memorial is … Algiers, … a hill. Answer: in Algiers, on a hill. Explanation: in for a city, on for a surface (a hill, a coast).
  2. Complete: The bookshop is … the bank … the pharmacy. Answer: between the bank and the pharmacy. Explanation: between needs two places joined by and.
  3. Locate on the map: Tamanrasset / Algeria / south. Answer: Tamanrasset is in the south of Algeria. Explanation: the town is inside the country, so in, not to.

🟠 Common BEM mistakes

  • Translating word for word: "on Algeria", "in the coast". Correct: in Algeria, on the coast.
  • Writing "in front" without of or "next" without to: in front of the school, next to the bank.
  • Using between with only one place: "between the bank". Add and + second place.
  • Confusing in the north of (inside) and to the north of (outside the place).

🟡 Quick review

  • in: country, city, room, box; on: street, coast, river, surface; at: point, address.
  • next to / beside, opposite, in front of, behind, between … and …, near.
  • in the north of (inside) ≠ to the north of (outside).
  • X is located / situated + preposition + place.
  • Question: Where is it? Where is it located?
