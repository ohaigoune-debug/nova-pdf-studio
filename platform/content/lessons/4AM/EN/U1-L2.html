The passive voice (present and past simple)

Goal: By the end of this lesson, you will be able to form and use the passive voice in the present simple and the past simple to describe landmarks: "The pyramids were built by the ancient Egyptians."

The idea in two lines: In the active voice the subject does the action; in the passive voice the subject receives it. The passive is formed with be + past participle, and we use it when the result matters more than the doer.

🔵 Rules and concepts

  1. Passive voice = subject + be + past participle (+ by + agent). The past participle is the third form of the verb: build – built – built, visit – visited – visited.
  2. Present simple passive: am / is / are + past participle. The Casbah is visited by thousands of tourists every year.
  3. Past simple passive: was / were + past participle. The Great Wall was built over many centuries.
  4. Agreement: be agrees with the new subject. The temple was restored but The temples were restored.
  5. The agent (by + doer) is often omitted when it is unknown, obvious or unimportant: The statue was damaged (by someone).
  6. Negative and question: The bridge wasn't finished in 1900. Was it designed by a French engineer?
  7. We use the passive to focus on the landmark, not on the people who built it.

🟢 Examples

  1. Turn into the passive: Tourists visit the Colosseum every day. Answer: The Colosseum is visited by tourists every day. Explanation: present simple, singular subject, so is + visited.
  2. Turn into the passive: The Ottomans built Ketchaoua Mosque in the 17th century. Answer: Ketchaoua Mosque was built by the Ottomans in the 17th century. Explanation: past simple, so was + built.
  3. Complete: Many ancient manuscripts … (keep) in the museum. Answer: are kept. Explanation: plural subject, present tense; the past participle of keep is kept.

🟠 Common BEM mistakes

  • Forgetting the verb be: "The mosque built in 1612" instead of The mosque was built in 1612.
  • Using the past simple form instead of the past participle: "was wrote" instead of was written.
  • Wrong agreement with the new subject: "The ruins was discovered" instead of were discovered.
  • Keeping the old subject: "The Colosseum was built the Romans" without by.

🟡 Quick review

  • Passive = be + past participle; the object becomes the subject.
  • Present: is / are + PP. Past: was / were + PP.
  • Check the agreement: singular → is / was; plural → are / were.
  • Add "by + agent" only when the doer is important.
  • Learn the irregular past participles: built, made, written, found, taken.
