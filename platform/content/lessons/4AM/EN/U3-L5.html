Expressing wishes and ambitions

Goal: To express wishes, hopes, dreams and ambitions with the right structures (I wish, I hope, I would like to, I dream of, My ambition is to) and to write a short paragraph about your future career.

The idea in two lines: A hope is something possible that you want to happen; a wish is often something unreal or difficult; an ambition is a strong goal you work towards. Each expression has its own grammar after it (infinitive with to, gerund, or a clause).

🔵 Rules and concepts

  1. I hope to + base verb / I hope (that) + clause: a realistic wish for the future. I hope to become a vet. I hope that I will pass the BEM.
  2. I would like to / I'd like to + base verb: a polite desire. I'd like to study architecture. Stronger: I'd love to.
  3. I wish + past simple / could: a wish about something that is not true now. I wish I were taller. I wish I could fly. (same logic as conditional type 2)
  4. I dream of + verb-ing / noun: She dreams of becoming a famous singer. Also: My dream is to + base verb.
  5. My ambition / goal / aim is to + base verb: My ambition is to open a hospital in my town. Also: I want to / I plan to / I intend to.
  6. Useful vocabulary: career, profession, achieve, succeed, reach a goal, make a dream come true.
  7. In a paragraph about your future, combine these expressions with will / going to and connectors (first, then, because).

🟢 Examples

  1. Complete: Yacine wants to be a footballer but he is short. He says: "I wish I ........ (be) taller." Solution: I wish I were taller. Explanation: the situation is unreal now, so wish + past simple.
  2. Join the ideas: Houda / dream / travel to Japan. Solution: Houda dreams of travelling to Japan. / Houda's dream is to travel to Japan.
  3. Write two sentences about your ambition. Model answer: My ambition is to become a computer engineer. I hope to study at a good university, so I'm going to work hard this year.

🟠 Common BEM mistakes

  • I hope become a doctor ✗ → I hope to become ✓.
  • I wish I will be rich ✗ → I wish I were rich ✓ (use hope for future possibilities, wish for unreal ones).
  • I dream to travel ✗ → I dream of travelling ✓ or My dream is to travel ✓.
  • Forgetting to after would like: I'd like be ✗.

🟡 Quick review

  • hope to + verb / hope that + clause (possible).
  • wish + past simple / could (unreal now).
  • would like to / would love to + verb.
  • dream of + verb-ing; my dream / ambition is to + verb.
  • Plan: want to, intend to, going to.
