Reported speech: statements

Goal: To report what a famous person said or wrote without quoting the exact words, by changing the tense, the pronouns and the time expressions correctly (reported statements only).

The idea in two lines: Direct speech repeats the exact words between quotation marks. Reported speech tells the same message with said (that) or told + person (that); when the reporting verb is in the past, the verb inside moves "one step back" into the past.

🔵 Rules and concepts

  1. Reporting verbs: say has no object (She said that...); tell needs a person (She told me that...). That can be left out.
  2. Tense change (backshift): present simple → past simple; present continuous → past continuous; past simple / present perfect → past perfect; will → would; can → could; must → had to.
  3. Pronouns change to fit the speaker: I → he / she; we → they; my → his / her; you → me / us / them depending on the listener.
  4. Time and place words move away from "now": today → that day; tomorrow → the next / following day; yesterday → the day before; now → then; here → there; this → that.
  5. If the reporting verb is in the present (He says that...), there is no tense change.
  6. Quotation marks, the capital letter and the final full stop of the quoted sentence disappear; the reported clause is a normal statement, not a question.

🟢 Examples

  1. Direct: The writer said, "I love my country and I write about its people." Solution: The writer said that he loved his country and he wrote about its people. Explanation: I → he, my → his, present simple → past simple.
  2. Direct: "I will visit the museum tomorrow," Amina told us. Solution: Amina told us that she would visit the museum the following day. Explanation: will → would; tomorrow → the following day.
  3. Direct: "We finished the project yesterday," the pupils said. Solution: The pupils said that they had finished the project the day before.

🟠 Common BEM mistakes

  • Writing She said me that ✗: use She told me that or She said to me that.
  • Forgetting to change the pronoun: He said that I was tired when the speaker was talking about himself ✗ → he was tired ✓.
  • Keeping the quotation marks or the question-word order in the reported sentence.
  • Changing the tense when the reporting verb is in the present (He says that he lives in Oran, not lived).

🟡 Quick review

  • said (that) / told + person (that).
  • One step back: present → past; past → past perfect; will → would; can → could.
  • I → he/she, we → they, my → his/her.
  • now → then, today → that day, tomorrow → the next day, here → there.
  • No quotation marks, no tense change after a present reporting verb.
