The passive voice

Goal: To turn active sentences into the passive voice in different tenses and with modals, and to know when the passive is preferred in scientific texts.

The idea in two lines: In the passive, the object of the active sentence becomes the subject, and the focus moves from the doer to the action. The form is always be + past participle, with be in the tense of the active verb.

🔵 Rules and concepts

  1. Form: object → subject + be (same tense) + past participle (+ by agent).
  2. Present / past simple: is / are observed; was / were observed.
  3. Continuous: is being built; was being built.
  4. Perfect: has / have been sent; had been sent.
  5. Future and modals: will / can / must / should + be + V3.
  6. Agent: omit by + agent when it is unknown, obvious or unimportant (by people, by someone).
  7. Impersonal passive: It is believed that… / The Moon is said to…

🟢 Examples

  1. Scientists are launching a new satellite. Solution: A new satellite is being launched (by scientists).
  2. They have sent robots to Mars. Solution: Robots have been sent to Mars. Explanation: they is a vague agent, so it is omitted.
  3. People say that the universe is expanding. Solution: It is said that the universe is expanding / The universe is said to be expanding.

🟠 Common BAC mistakes

  • Changing the tense: the tense of be must match the active verb.
  • Forgetting agreement: The stars was seen → were seen.
  • Using the past simple form instead of the past participle: was builded / was wrote.
  • Keeping by them / by people when the agent is vague.

🟡 Quick review

  • Passive = be (active tense) + V3.
  • Continuous: being; perfect: been.
  • Modal + be + V3.
  • Drop vague agents; use It is said that… for opinions.
